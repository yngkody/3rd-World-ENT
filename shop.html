SHIPPING AND FULFILLMENT POLICY

Order Processing: Ready-to-ship items are normally processed within 2 to 4 business days. Made-to-order, torched, and individually hand-finished items are normally processed within 7 to 14 business days. Business days do not include weekends or holidays.

Orders with Different Fulfillment Times: If one order contains both ready-to-ship and made-to-order items, split shipping is offered so your in-stock pieces arrive as soon as possible without delay.

Domestic Delivery: Standard delivery within the United States is estimated at 3 to 7 business days after shipment. Expedited delivery estimates apply only after processing. Free standard shipping applies to orders over $120.

Tracking Information: A shipping confirmation with tracking information will be sent when the package is accepted by the carrier. Tracking may take up to 48 hours to update.

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES POLICY

Eligible Ready to Ship Items: A size exchange or store-credit return may be requested within 14 days of delivery for an eligible ready-to-ship item. The item must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, free of odor, and returned in its original condition.

Final Sale Items: Made-to-order pieces, torched pieces, discounted items, and items marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged. Natural differences in hand distressing or torch placement are intentional and not defects.

Damaged or Incorrect Items: Report an incorrect or damaged item within 5 days of delivery by emailing support@3rdworldent.com with photos of the item and original packaging.

GARMENT CARE PROTOCOL:

Turn the garment inside out before washing. Use cold water and mild detergent. Hand wash torched and heavily distressed pieces when possible. Do not use bleach. Air dry — avoid machine heat which can widen distressed openings.
