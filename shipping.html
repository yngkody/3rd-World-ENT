SHIPPING AND FULFILLMENT POLICY

SECTION: POL-FUL-2025
01. ORDER PROCESSING

Ready-to-ship items are normally processed within 2 to 4 business days. Made-to-order, torched, and individually hand-finished items are normally processed within 7 to 14 business days. Business days do not include weekends or holidays.

During launches, restocks, or periods of unusually high order volume, processing may take longer. Carrier transit time begins only after an order has been completed, packed, and accepted by the carrier.

02. SPLIT & COMBINED FULFILLMENT

If one order contains both ready-to-ship and made-to-order items, the full order will normally ship together when every item is ready. If split shipping is offered, the option and any additional shipping charge will appear at checkout.

03. DOMESTIC DELIVERY

Standard delivery within the United States is generally estimated at 3 to 7 business days after shipment. Expedited delivery estimates, when available, apply only after processing and do not shorten made-to-order production time. Delivery dates are estimates and are not guaranteed.

04. INTERNATIONAL DELIVERY & CUSTOMS

If international shipping is available to your destination at checkout, delivery commonly takes 7 to 21 business days after shipment. Customs processing can cause additional delays. The customer is responsible for any import duties, taxes, or brokerage fees charged by the destination country.

05. TRACKING INFORMATION

A shipping confirmation with tracking information will be sent when the package is accepted by the carrier. Tracking may take up to 48 hours to update.

06. SHIPPING ADDRESS VERIFICATION

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate shipping address. Email support@3rdworldent.com immediately if an address needs to be corrected. An address change cannot be guaranteed after fulfillment begins.

07. MISSING OR DAMAGED CARRIER PACKAGES

If tracking shows delivered but the package is missing, check the delivery area, household members, neighbors, and the carrier first. Allow 48 hours because some carriers mark packages delivered before final placement. If the package still cannot be located, contact support within 7 days. If a package arrives visibly damaged, photograph the packaging and item within 5 days.

08. RETURNED SHIPMENTS

Orders returned because of an incomplete address, refused delivery, or failed delivery attempt may require a new shipping payment before they are sent again. Original shipping charges are not refundable.
